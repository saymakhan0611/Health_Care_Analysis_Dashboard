# Health_Care_Analysis_Dashboard
## I have developed an interactive Healthcare Analysis Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel to analyse patient information, hospital revenue, treatments, medical conditions, and overall hospital performance. This process involved several stages, including data pre-processing, data cleaning, data visualization.

## Dataset used
- <a href="https://github.com/saymakhan0611/Health_Care_Analysis_Dashboard/blob/main/Dataset.xlsx">Dataset</a>

### Key KPIs
- Number of Patients – Displays the distinct number of patients based on the selected filters. 
- Total Revenue – Shows the total hospital revenue generated from patient billing. 
- Average Bill Amount – Calculates the average bill amount per patient. 
-	Highest Bill Amount – Identifies the maximum bill amount recorded. 
-	Average Length of Stay – Shows the average number of days patients stay in the hospital. 
-	Unique Medical Conditions – Counts the number of different medical conditions. 
-	Unique Treatment Types – Shows the number of different treatments. 
-	Average Patient Age – Calculates the average age of patients.

### Process Description
1. Data Collection
Patient and hospital-related data was collected, including Patient ID, Age, Gender, Medical Condition, Treatment, Admission Date, Discharge Date, Bill Amount, and Length of Stay.
2. Data Cleaning & Preparation
The raw dataset was cleaned in Excel/Power Query by removing duplicate records, handling missing values, correcting data types, and standardizing fields such as dates, gender, medical conditions, and treatments.
3. Data Transformation
Additional fields such as Year, Month, Age Group, and Length of Stay were created to make the data suitable for analysis and time-based reporting.
4. KPI Development
Key performance indicators were created to provide a quick overview of hospital performance:
- Total/Distinct Patients
- Total Revenue
- Average Bill Amount
- Average Length of Stay
- Number of Medical Conditions
5. Pivot Table Analysis
PivotTables were used to analyze patients, revenue, billing, treatments, medical conditions, gender, and yearly trends.
6. Dashboard Visualization
Charts were created to visually represent important insights, such as:
- Patients by Medical Condition
- Revenue by Year
- Patients by Gender
- Revenue by Medical Condition
- Patients by Treatment
- Average Bill by Treatment
- Average Length of Stay trends
7. Interactive Filtering
Slicers and Timeline were added for interactive analysis. Users can filter the dashboard by Year, Month, Gender, Medical Condition, and other relevant fields.
8. Insight Generation
The final dashboard allows users to quickly identify trends in patient volume, hospital revenue, treatment demand, billing patterns, and patient stay duration, supporting better hospital performance analysis and decision-making.
